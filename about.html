About

Hydrology PhD candidate working at the pore scale.

I am a PhD candidate at National Taiwan University, advised by Prof. Shao-Yiu Hsu. My dissertation is paper-based: it combines my first-author journal articles on the displacement and separation of resident ("old") and invading ("new") wetting liquids in micromodels.

Before the micromodel work, I studied bubble entrapment in pore-doublet models and the effect of ponding height on suction-head estimation in the Green–Ampt infiltration model. I have also contributed to team projects on soil-on-a-chip fabrication, drying of porous media, lysimeter clogging, and agricultural water accounting.

Methods & tools

  • Microfluidic micromodel design and experiments (pore-doublet and grain-based patterns)
  • Image processing of dye-tracer displacement experiments
  • Analytical pore-doublet models; direct numerical simulation of two-phase flow
  • Infiltration and unsaturated-flow modelling
