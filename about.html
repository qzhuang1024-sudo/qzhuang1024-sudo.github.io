About

Hydrologist working from pores to catchments.

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Bioenvironmental Systems Engineering, National Taiwan University, Taiwan, advised by Prof. Shao-Yiu Hsu. I received my B.S. from the Department of Earth Sciences and my M.S. from the Graduate Institute of Hydrological and Oceanic Sciences, both at National Central University (NCU), which gave me a solid foundation in geophysics and hydrology.

My current research focuses on soil water dynamics at the Darcy and pore scales. My paper-based dissertation investigates the separation of old and new wetting liquids in micromodels. I have also participated in research projects on irrigation and water-resource management under the impacts of climate change.

Methods

  • Microfluidic micromodel experiments (pore-doublet and grain-based patterns)
  • Pore-doublet theory and direct numerical simulation of wetting-fluid displacement
  • Infiltration modelling (Green–Ampt) and tensiometry
  • Electrical resistivity tomography (ERT) and groundwater monitoring in paddy fields
  • Statistical streamflow forecasting from long-term weather outlooks
