Qun-Zhan Huang

PhD Candidate · Department of Bioenvironmental Systems Engineering, National Taiwan University

I study soil water dynamics at the Darcy and pore scales. My dissertation uses microfluidic micromodels to observe how old and new wetting liquids displace, mix and separate in porous media — the pore-scale basis of the ecohydrological separation hypothesis. I also work on irrigation and water-resource management under climate change, from terraced paddy fields to streamflow forecasting.
