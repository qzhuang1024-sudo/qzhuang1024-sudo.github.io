Qun-Zhan Huang

PhD Candidate · National Taiwan University

I study how old and new wetting liquids displace, mix, and separate inside porous media, using microfluidic micromodels and pore-scale modelling. The work is motivated by the ecohydrological separation ("two water worlds") hypothesis — why water taken up by plants can differ isotopically from the water that drains to streams.
