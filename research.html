Research

Soil water dynamics at the Darcy and pore scales — and what they mean for water management.

1 · Old and new water in the pore space (dissertation)

Stable-isotope field studies suggest that water held in small pores ("old" water) can remain distinct from newly infiltrating water. I use transparent micromodels to observe directly how these waters interact.

Pore-doublet micromodels: evaporation and pre-event water entrapment

How evaporation before a wetting event controls pre-event water entrapment and pre-event/event water interaction.

Huang et al. (2023), Advances in Water Resources.

Grain-based pattern micromodels: separation during wetting cycles

Tracking separation of old and new wetting liquids across repeated wetting cycles in realistic grain geometries.

Huang et al. (2024), International Agrophysics.

Related pore-scale work

Direct numerical simulation of wetting–wetting displacement (Wang et al., 2023, Water); air entrapment in 2D/2.5D soil-on-a-chip micromodels (Tsao et al., 2021, Lab on a Chip); Euler characteristic during drying (Shih et al., 2021, Drying Technology).

2 · Infiltration and unsaturated-zone measurement

Ponding height and the Green–Ampt model

Investigating and parameterizing how ponding height affects dynamic suction-head estimation.

Huang et al. (2023), Journal of Hydrology. Related: tensiometer response time (Tsai et al., 2020, J. Hydrol.); lysimeter clogging (Lo et al., 2022, Land).

3 · Irrigation and water resources under climate change

Subsurface flow under terraced paddy fields

Electrical resistivity tomography and groundwater-level monitoring beneath an irrigated terraced paddy field in Northern Taiwan.

Huang et al. (2024), Paddy and Water Environment.

Streamflow exceedance probability from long-term weather outlooks

Logistic-regression forecasts of streamflow exceedance probability for water-supply planning in Northern Taiwan.

Huang et al. (2026), Terrestrial, Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences.
